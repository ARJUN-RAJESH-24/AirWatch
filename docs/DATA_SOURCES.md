# Authoritative Data Sources & Provenance Documentation

AirWatch relies exclusively on authoritative environmental observations collected by official Indian government agencies. No simulated, predicted, or synthetic data is presented as actual environmental readings.

---

## 1. Primary Data Providers

### 1.1 Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
- **Authority**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEF&CC), Government of India
- **Program**: Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring System (CAAQMS)
- **Official Portal**: [https://airquality.cpcb.gov.in/AQI_India/](https://airquality.cpcb.gov.in/AQI_India/)
- **National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS)**: Gazette of India Notification No. B-29016/20/90/PCI-L, dated November 18, 2009.
- **Coverage**: 230 continuous ambient stations spanning 21 Indian States and Union Territories.
- **Acquired Assets**:
  - `cpcb_stations.csv` (14.4 KB) — Master station registry with geographic coordinates, state, and city designations.
  - `cpcb_station_day.csv` (8.63 MB) — Daily continuous ambient observations covering 2015 to 2020.
  - Cryptographic Manifest: `cpcb_station_day.csv.provenance.json` (SHA-256: `95574c8ea54d76f030eb9c5e3f5fb47c5d3367129ee79e273012a6327ee3ca48`).

### 1.2 Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India (`data.gov.in`)
- **Authority**: National Informatics Centre (NIC), Ministry of Electronics & IT, Government of India
- **Catalogue**: Real-Time Air Quality Index (Resource ID: `3b01bcb8-0b14-4abf-b6f2-c1bfd384ba69`)
- **API Endpoint**: `https://api.data.gov.in/resource/3b01bcb8-0b14-4abf-b6f2-c1bfd384ba69`
- **Access Model**: Requires an API Key generated by registering an account on `data.gov.in`. Configured via `DATAGOVIN_API_KEY`.
- **Query Limits**: 10,000 requests per day per API key; 10 requests per second rate limit.
- **License**: Government Open Data License - India (GODL-India).

### 1.3 Kerala State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB)
- **Authority**: Department of Environment, Government of Kerala
- **Official Portal**: [https://kspcb.kerala.gov.in/](https://kspcb.kerala.gov.in/)
- **Historical Catalogue**: [https://kerala.data.gov.in/catalog/historical-daily-ambient-air-quality-data](https://kerala.data.gov.in/catalog/historical-daily-ambient-air-quality-data)
- **Monitoring Scope**: All 8 official continuous monitoring stations in Kerala are prioritized and fully cataloged:
  1. `KL001`: Eloor, Kochi (Industrial area)
  2. `KL002`: Kariavattom, Thiruvananthapuram (Institutional area)
  3. `KL003`: MG Road, Kochi (Commercial corridor)
  4. `KL004`: Vyttila, Kochi (Traffic intersection)
  5. `KL005`: Polayathode, Kollam (Urban residential)
  6. `KL006`: Palayam, Kozhikode (Commercial center)
  7. `KL007`: Irumpanam, Kochi (Petrochemical zone)
  8. `KL008`: Plammoodu, Thiruvananthapuram (Urban residential)

## 4. Data Integrity & Cleaning Policy

1. **Null vs Zero Preservation**: Unreported or offline sensor values in official CSV exports are left empty or null. The pipeline never replaces missing readings with zero, as zero concentration represents pristine air and would falsify statistical averages.
2. **Quality Tagging**:
   - `VALID`: Measured concentration falls within physical measurement limits.
   - `ANOMALY`: Extreme outlier value exceeding physical bounds (e.g. negative concentration or reading exceeding sensor saturation threshold).
3. **Idempotent Imports**: Each observation record receives a deterministic SHA-256 fingerprint:
   $$\text{Fingerprint} = \text{SHA256}(\text{station\_code} \parallel \text{timestamp} \parallel \text{pollutant\_id})$$
   Re-importing source files updates existing rows without producing duplicated rows.
4. **Known Limitations**:
   - Historical CAAQMS datasets provide daily averaged records rather than 15-minute raw sensor telemetry.
   - Earlier monitoring stations in 2015-2017 have periodic data gaps due to sensor recalibration or power interruption.
